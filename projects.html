Invoke-CompromiseCheck.ps1

PurposeSpeed up first-response triage on suspected Windows compromises.

DoneWrote a PowerShell tool that automates about 20 manual IR commands: RDP and failed logons, Defender status, suspicious PowerShell, hosts-file tampering, remote-access software. Colour-coded output and a timestamped report.

Result20 checks in about 13 seconds (8-check quick mode). It flagged a Defender false positive that Microsoft later acknowledged and fixed.

CTI Dashboard

PurposeKeep a daily, structured view of the threat landscape.

DoneBuilt a seven-layer dashboard in Inoreader (63 feeds): news, IOCs/CVEs, hunt queries, actor context, reference, community and breach awareness, plus a DACH layer. Uses AlienVault OTX, abuse.ch and MITRE ATT&CK.

ResultRead daily; used to triage the Storm-1865 phishing campaign.
