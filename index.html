About me
Eight years running infrastructure taught me how it breaks.
Keeping enterprise systems alive is the fastest way to learn exactly where — and how — they fail.

The path

My career began in IT operations and infrastructure — running Active Directory, endpoint security, and identity systems for a FINMA-regulated Swiss bank, Microsoft Switzerland, and international schools. I learned how enterprise environments are really built, secured, and where they quietly fail.

The transition

I'm now completing a federal Cybersecurity Specialist diploma at the Swiss Cyber Institute, with CEH in progress. I moved deliberately from keeping systems running to understanding how they're attacked and defended — the two halves of the same problem.

Hands-on practice

I build and document my own labs — SIEM deployment, DFIR reconstruction, memory forensics, web-app security — investigate real-world threats, and practice on Hack The Box, TryHackMe, PortSwigger, and CyberDefenders. I learn by doing and write up what I find.
